# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Lifecycle.NET community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and acknowledge mistakes.
- Report unacceptable behavior privately and promptly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, hateful language, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, reject, or edit contributions and communications that violate this Code of Conduct. Report concerns through the contact route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially where practical.
